## 日本語

### タイトル
夜明けの楔（よあけのくさび）

### ひとこと（短い説明・約 100 字）
同じ夜をくり返す時計塔で、「時の楔」を手に夜明けを目指す 2D アクション・ローグライト。倒れても、知ったことと楔は次の夜に残る。

### 紹介文
鐘が鳴るたび、夜がはじめに巻き戻る――。
あなたは時計塔の主「刻守」の弟子。町が同じ夜をくり返していることを覚えていられるのは、師から「時の楔」を習ったあなただけ。
けれど時戻しの鐘は禁忌の技。鳴らすたびに、師の命は一夜ぶん削られていく。

市場、歯車回廊、鐘楼。夜ごとに組み替わる塔を登り、敵を斬り、強化を選び、師のもとへ。
倒れても無駄にはならない。手に入れた知識と楔、刻んだ刻印は次の夜に持ち越され、塔の運命を少しずつ変えていく。

師はなぜ、命を削ってまで夜を止めるのか。
くり返しの先にある、ほんとうの夜明けを見つけてください。

### 特徴
- **くり返すほど強くなる**：倒れても、知識・楔・刻印は次の夜へ。夜を重ねるたびに、行ける場所と打てる手が増えていく
- **選んで育てる戦い方**：100 種類以上の強化。組み合わせで合成・共鳴し、毎回ちがう戦い方になる
- **小さなお供**：3 匹から選べるお供が、そばで一緒に戦い、レベルに合わせて進化する
- **気持ちのいい手ざわり**：連撃・術・回避。ぎりぎりで避けると、すぐ次の回避ができる「ジャスト回避」
- **5 段階の難しさ**：「めちゃ簡単」から「激ムズ」まで。アクションが苦手な人も、腕に自信のある人も、自分に合った難しさで最後まで遊べる
- **4 つの結末と、終わらない夜**：結末を見たあとも、強化を引き継いで遊び続けられる
- **ドット絵と、すべて手づくりの音**：絵・音楽・効果音はすべてプログラムで生成
- **遊びやすさの設定**：文字の大きさ（3 段階）、画面揺れの切り替え、光の点滅を抑える設定
- **キーボード・ゲームパッド・タッチ操作**に対応

## English

### Title
Wedge of Dawn

### Short description
A 2D action roguelite set in a clock tower where the same night repeats forever. Wield the "Wedge of Time" and fight your way toward dawn. Fall, and your knowledge and wedges carry into the next night.

### About the game
Every time the bell tolls, the night rewinds to its beginning.
You are the apprentice of the Timekeeper, master of the clock tower. Thanks to the "Wedge of Time" your master taught you, you alone remember that the town is reliving the same night.
But the rewinding bell is a forbidden art. Each time it rings, it costs your master one night of life.

Climb a tower that reshapes itself each night: the market, the gear corridors, the belfry. Cut down enemies, choose your upgrades, and face your master.
No death is wasted. The knowledge you gain, the wedges you drive and the crests you carve all carry over, slowly changing the fate of the tower.

Why would your master give up their life to hold back the dawn?
Find the true dawn that waits beyond the loop.

### Features
- **Grow stronger with every loop**: knowledge, wedges and crests carry over. Each night opens new paths and new options.
- **Build your own style**: 100+ upgrades that fuse and resonate into a different build every run.
- **A little companion**: pick one of three companions who fight by your side and evolve as you level up.
- **Satisfying combat**: combos, arts and dodges. Dodge at the last moment for a Perfect Dodge that instantly refreshes your dodge.
- **Five difficulty levels**: from Very Easy to Nightmare. Whether you're new to action games or a veteran, play to the end at your own pace.
- **Four endings and an endless night**: keep playing after the ending with your upgrades intact.
- **Pixel art with fully procedural audio**: every sprite, song and sound effect is generated by code.
- **Accessibility options**: three text sizes, screen shake toggle, and a reduced-flashing mode.
- **Keyboard, gamepad and touch controls** supported.
- *Text is currently in Japanese only.*

### AI disclosure
The program, art, music, sound effects and text of this game were created with generative AI (Claude and ChatGPT). The original concept, direction and playtesting decisions were made by a human.

### Photosensitivity warning
This game contains flashing lights and screen shake, both of which can be reduced in the settings. If you have a history of photosensitive epilepsy, consult a doctor before playing.
